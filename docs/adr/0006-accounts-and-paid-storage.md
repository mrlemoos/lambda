# ADR 0006: Accounts and paid storage

Local-only positioning (“nothing leaves your machine”; auth out of scope in ADR 0002 / ADR 0004) is withdrawn. **Lambda Web** and desktop share one **Account**. Sign-in is required to write except **first-run offline**: this client has a cached **Lambda Web** shell, is offline, and has never signed in. Empty cache + offline cannot write (Electron does not ship the Next.js bundle). After any successful sign-in, that client keeps the Account (cached session when offline; 30-day sliding — ADR 0014). Online with no session is a sign-in wall. **Account** credentials are **email and password** (better-auth); OAuth, magic link, and passkeys are out of scope for this effort. Unverified email may write locally; email verification is required before **Paid storage**. Password reset is in scope. **Paid storage** keeps a **Script** on Lambda’s store as a CRDT (see ADR 0011) and includes realtime collaboration and comments (not a second product). Signed-in without paid storage writes locally only. First sign-in keeps **First-run offline** library entries as local-only on that client (no auto-Store). Purchase is Stripe at **Store** (see ADR 0012). Fountain remains the interchange format. Invites to collaborate are only for people who already have an **Account** (see ADR 0013). **Account** rows and sessions persist in Neon Postgres (see ADR 0008).

**Date:** 2026-08-17
