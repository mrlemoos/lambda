# ADR 0012: Stripe at Store, not at sign-up

**Paid storage** is bought with **Stripe** (Vercel Marketplace on execute). The paywall appears when the author **Store**s a **Script**, not on sign-up and not on local write. One SKU: store includes **Collaboration**. Quota and price numbers stay product-later.

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-18
