# ADR 0013: Invites go to existing Accounts

**Collaboration** invites are only for people who already have a Lambda **Account**. The owner picks by **Account** email in-app. The invitee sees it the next time they open **Lambda Web**. A notify email to that address is allowed. They need not have opened Lambda Web or installed desktop before the invite. No public links. No inviting emails that are not Accounts.

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-18
